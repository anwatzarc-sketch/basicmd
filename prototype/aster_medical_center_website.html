FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-in patients?

Yes, walk-ins are welcome for general consultations, though advance appointment requests are recommended to minimize waiting times.

How are appointment requests confirmed?

Our intake coordinator reviews your requested slot and contacts you by phone or SMS to confirm your exact appointment time.

Do you provide 24/7 emergency services?

Yes. Our emergency support team is active 24/7. For immediate assistance, please call our emergency hotline directly.

Can I request care in Amharic?

Yes, all our clinical staff and patient service representatives fluently communicate in Amharic and English.
